    How seats are marked won. Élections Québec does not declare winners on election night. This page marks a riding won when every polling station has reported, when the leader's margin is larger than all ballots plausibly left to count, or when the lead is safe for the share of polls still out (at least 15% counted; the margin needed falls from about 24 points at 15% counted to about 3 points at 90%). TV networks use their own models and may call ridings earlier or differently.
